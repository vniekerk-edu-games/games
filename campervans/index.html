We are the Van Niekerks, a family of five – Mom, Dad and three boys – who are passionate about camping and the great outdoors. We started with a simple list of things to remember about different campsites, from facilities to the best spots. When we realised how helpful this could be for other campers, we decided to share it.

Over time our friends added their favourite campsites and tips, and our community of campers grew. We hope this site helps you find the perfect spot for your next adventure.
